Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Next Tower ("the game") is made by Baihaqi Studio ("we"). This page explains what information the game and this website use. It covers the game on Android and this website.

The short version

  • The game plays fully offline.
  • We collect no personal data. There are no accounts and no servers of our own.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress is saved on your own device.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information on your device

The game saves your progress in its own storage on your device: planes and days cleared, hero levels, town buildings, items and settings. It is not sent anywhere. You can delete it by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Purchases

Plane 1 is free. The rest of the game unlocks with one one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; the game receives only a confirmation that the purchase went through (an order reference and the product bought) so it can unlock the game and restore it after a reinstall. We never receive your card or payment details. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, Google Play may send an anonymous technical report (device model, Android version, the error) to us through the Google Play Console, depending on your device's diagnostics settings. These reports do not identify you and are used only to fix problems.

Device backup

If your Android device backs up app data to your Google account, your save may be included. Google runs that backup under your account and settings; we cannot see it.

Permissions

The game uses internet access only for purchases through Google Play. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other files.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version uses information in a new way, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
